# Code of Conduct

This project should be a respectful, practical place to collaborate.

## Expected Behavior

- Be considerate, direct, and constructive.
- Assume good intent while staying accountable for impact.
- Keep technical debate focused on evidence, safety, and maintainability.
- Respect privacy, confidentiality, and legal constraints around data,
  experiments, equipment, and institutions.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, intimidation, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing private information, credentials, confidential data, or restricted
  technical details without permission.
- Pressuring maintainers or contributors to disclose unsafe, proprietary, or
  legally restricted information.
- Deliberately submitting malicious code, unsafe experiment guidance, or
  misleading results.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues or pull requests, block users, or
take other reasonable actions to protect the project and its contributors.

Report conduct concerns through a private maintainer contact channel listed on
the repository profile or organization page.
